iPhone Install with TestFlight

TestFlight is Apple's free app for trying apps before they're released. Needs iOS 15 or later.

  1. Install TestFlight from the App Store.
    Get TestFlight
  2. Open the ResQ invite link on your iPhone.
    Join the ResQ beta
    If it says the beta isn't accepting testers yet, Apple is still reviewing it. Try again later.
  3. Tap Accept, then Install. ResQ appears on your home screen with an orange dot next to its name. The dot marks a test app.

Invited by email instead?

If we added you as an internal tester, you don't need the link above, and you can install right away without waiting for Apple's review.

  1. Accept the App Store Connect invite. Open the email from Apple titled "You've been invited to App Store Connect" and tap Accept invitation. Sign in with the Apple ID on your iPhone. Can't find it? Check your spam folder.
  2. Install TestFlight from the App Store if you haven't already.
  3. Open the TestFlight invite. A second email, "You're invited to test ResQ", arrives once we add you to the test group. Open it on your iPhone and tap View in TestFlight, then Install. The second email can take a little while after you accept the first one.

Android Install the APK

The test version isn't on the Play Store yet, so you install the file directly. Needs Android 5.0 or later.

  1. Download ResQ-1.0.0.apk on your phone.
    Download ResQ for Android (60 MB)
    Opens in your browser and downloads the file. Use Wi-Fi if you can.
  2. Open the file from your notifications or the Downloads folder.
  3. If you see "For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps", tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then go back. This applies only to the app you opened the file from, like Chrome, Files or Messenger.
  4. Tap Install. If Google Play Protect warns about an unknown app, tap More details → Install anyway. It warns about any app that isn't from the Play Store.

After you install

The same on iPhone and Android.

  1. Register with your Philippine mobile number and enter the code we text you.
  2. Answer the health screening. It decides whether you can donate now. Answer honestly; it's the same check a blood bank does.
  3. Tap Allow when ResQ asks to send notifications. That's how you'll hear about urgent requests for your blood type.
  4. Explore and try things out: the dashboard, booking an appointment, your profile and Digital Health Card, and Face ID or fingerprint sign-in in Settings.
Everything in this beta is a test. The hospitals, blood requests and alerts you see are test data, not real emergencies. Feel free to book appointments and try everything; no hospital is expecting you.

If something goes wrong

"This beta is full" or "isn't accepting testers"
The tester limit was reached or the link was paused. Message us and we'll open more spots.
The iPhone app stopped opening
Each test build lasts 90 days. Open TestFlight and install the newest build.
Android says "App not installed"
Free up some storage, or delete an older ResQ test version first, then open the file again.
I didn't get the text code
Wait a minute and tap resend. Check that you typed your number correctly, starting with 09 or +63.
No notifications
Check that notifications for ResQ are on in your phone's Settings, and in ResQ → Settings → Push App Notifications.
Updates iPhone: TestFlight installs new builds for you, or tap Update in TestFlight. Android: we'll share a new APK, and it installs over the old one without losing your account.
